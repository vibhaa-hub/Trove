Trove 

Business, Functional, Technical, Security & Discovery Blueprint 

1. Executive Summary 

Trove is an AI-powered Inheritance Operating System enabling families, financial institutions, executors, trustees, and beneficiaries to: 

Plan inheritance before death 

Preserve critical documents 

Register assets 

Assign beneficiaries 

Discover unknown assets 

Validate legal heirs 

Prevent fraud 

Orchestrate claims 

Facilitate settlement 

The platform supports two operating modes: 

Plan Mode (Pre-Death) 

The individual proactively registers assets and inheritance intentions. 

Discovery Mode (Post-Death) 

The individual never registered with Trove and assets must be discovered through documents, emails, institutions, government records, and AI-driven investigation. 

Both modes operate on the same: 

Data model 

Knowledge graph 

Security framework 

AI agents 

Integrations 

Workflows 

Compliance controls
