# United Kingdom: official tracing routes (verify current availability)
- Bank and building-society accounts: My Lost Account (mylostaccount.org.uk), free.
- Pensions: Pension Tracing Service (gov.uk/find-pension-contact-details).
- Policies, pensions, shares: Unclaimed Assets Register (paid search).
- Property: HM Land Registry title search.
- Government departments: Tell Us Once.
