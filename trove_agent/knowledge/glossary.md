# Inheritance glossary

## Nominee
The person an account holder names to receive the money when they die. In many
jurisdictions a nominee is a trustee who receives the funds but must pass them
to the legal heirs; nomination is not the same as inheritance.

## Legal heir
A person entitled to inherit under the will or, without a will, under
succession law. Legal heirship is decided by law and courts, not by Trove.

## Executor
The person named in a will to administer the estate: collect assets, pay debts,
and distribute what remains according to the will.

## Probate
Court confirmation that a will is valid and that the executor may act.
Whether probate is required depends on the jurisdiction and the assets.

## Succession certificate / letters of administration
Court documents that authorise someone to collect debts and securities, or to
administer an estate, when there is no will or no executor.

## Transmission
Moving securities or fund units from a deceased holder to a nominee or heir.

## Proof of life (check-in)
In Trove, the owner periodically confirms they are alive. A check-in cancels
any pending death claim immediately.
