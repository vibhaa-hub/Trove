# Claiming by asset type

General guidance only. Exact requirements depend on the institution, the
amount and the jurisdiction. Always ask the institution for its current
deceased-claim checklist.

## Bank accounts and deposits
Contact the bank's bereavement or deceased-claims desk. Usually needed: a
certified death certificate, the claimant's ID and address proof, the account
reference, and a claim form. If a nominee is registered on the account, the
bank normally pays the nominee, who holds the money for the legal heirs. If no
nominee is registered, banks may ask for a succession certificate, letters of
administration or probate above a threshold, or an indemnity and affidavit for
smaller amounts.

## Life insurance
Notify the insurer quickly; many policies have claim time limits. Needed: the
policy number, death certificate, claim form, claimant ID, bank details for
payment, and sometimes medical records or a police report for accidental death.
Group life cover through an employer is claimed via the employer's HR team.

## Mutual funds and shares
Transmission (not transfer) moves units or shares to the nominee or legal heir.
Contact the registrar or depository participant with the death certificate,
transmission form, claimant KYC, and the folio or demat account number. Without
a nominee, legal heir documents are usually required.

## Pensions and provident funds
Contact the employer and the pension or provident fund administrator. Ask about
the fund balance, any family pension, gratuity, unpaid salary and leave
encashment. Keep the member number (for example UAN or PRAN) ready.

## Property
Property passes by will or succession law. Ownership records are updated by
mutation or transfer at the land or municipal registry, normally after probate
or a succession document. Keep paying property tax and insurance meanwhile.

## Loans and credit cards
Inform every lender in writing. Debts are generally paid from the estate, not
personally by family members, unless someone was a co-borrower or guarantor.
Ask whether loan-protection insurance covers the balance.

## Crypto assets
Exchange accounts can be claimed through the exchange's deceased-user process.
Self-custody wallets can only be recovered with the seed phrase or keys. Never
share a seed phrase with anyone, and keep it offline.
